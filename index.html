02

The person

Background, the way he works, and where the aim is pointed.

I am creative on purpose. I lead when the work needs a leader, and I speak so a room stays with me. I try things, and I learn them. I do not treat failure as a defeat. I treat it as a lesson — and I am aimed at greater heights.

The primary study is at the University of Colombo: a postgraduate in tourism economics, finished, and a master’s in economics now underway. Beside it sits computing at LNBTI, Lanka Nippon BizTech Institute — the diploma on the degree pathway — and Japanese, practiced until it is fluent. Hospitality, events, economics, and systems are not separate lives. They are one person, learning in both directions.

05

Three systems

Built small, on purpose. A shop, a counter, a ward.

Python

Hardware shop management

A Python system for a hardware shop. Items and prices, stock moving in and out, and a clear view of what the counter sold before the shelf runs quiet.

Java

Point of sale

A Java point of sale for the counter itself. A bill built line by line, quantities beside each item, and a total a cashier can trust before the next person steps up.

MySQL

Hospital equipment register

A MySQL database for hospital equipment. Which place holds a piece, whether it is in use or spare, and what has to be maintained before someone needs it again.
